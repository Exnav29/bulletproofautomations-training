bpa-reading-400.woff2 and bpa-reading-700.woff2 are subsets of Andika by SIL International,
licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (Andika-OFL.txt). Because "Andika" is a
Reserved Font Name and subsetting is a modification, the subsets are renamed
"BPA Reading" as the licence requires. Characters kept: Latin, Latin Extended,
Akan/Ewe letters (U+0186 U+0189 U+0190 U+0254 U+0256 U+025B), combining marks for
Yoruba tone, U+1E00-1EFF, the cedi and naira signs.

eb-garamond-600.woff2 is a 600-weight instance and subset of EB Garamond
(EBGaramond-OFL.txt), which declares no Reserved Font Name.
