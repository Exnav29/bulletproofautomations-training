# Participant materials: Get Paid for Automation, workshop 01

"You know automation. Do you know what to charge?" (Saturday 12 December 2026)

These files are for registered participants. They are **not published on the website**: `content/`
is outside the build allowlist in `scripts/build.js`. Share them during or after the workshop, for
example as attachments to a follow-up email.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `pricing-worksheet.xlsx` | The worksheet used in the workshop and in the pricing clinic. It works in Excel and Google Sheets. |
| `quotation-template.docx` | An editable project quotation. Participants replace the bracketed text and send it as a PDF. |
| `build_materials.py` | Rebuilds both files: `py build_materials.py` (needs `openpyxl` and `python-docx`). Edit this file, not the outputs. |

## The worksheet, sheet by sheet

The sheets map to the workshop agenda.

| Sheet | Workshop segment | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Your costs | 0:15 | Monthly business costs, the earnings you need, and billable capacity, giving a minimum hourly rate (the floor) |
| 2 Project estimate | 0:15 to 1:00 | Hours per phase, including discovery, testing, revisions, handover and communication; risk contingency; support; margin. Gives the hourly price and the fixed-project price |
| 3 Value check | 0:15 to 1:00 | What the client's problem costs per year, the realistic first-year value, and the client's payback period |
| 4 Price decision | 1:00 | Hourly, fixed and value-informed prices side by side, a check against cost, and a deposit schedule |
| 5 Pricing clinic | 2:00 | Three fictional practice projects: a Kumasi wholesaler, an Accra clinic group and an overseas agency priced in US dollars |
| 6 Pricing mistakes | Throughout | Ten common mistakes and the fix for each (learning outcome 4) |

Yellow cells are inputs and green cells are calculated. The example figures make the sheet show
working numbers. They are illustrations, not market rates, and the file says so. The example quoted
price on sheet 4 is deliberately below cost so the check fires.

## Owner review before use

- Read the three clinic scenarios and adjust any detail that does not fit your experience.
- The overseas example asks participants to enter today's exchange rate themselves. No rate is
  built in.
- The worksheet names a tax set-aside but no rate, and says to ask a tax adviser.
