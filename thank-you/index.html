What you have paid for

The five-week Intermediate cohort, and — if you chose the bundle — one attempt at the BCAB assessment.

Training and certification are separate products. Completing the cohort qualifies you to attempt the assessment. It does not award the credential, and no payment can. The credential is earned by passing.

If you do not reach the standard first time you receive written feedback and an outcome of Assessment Not Yet Passed or Remediation Required, and you may reattempt. Every attempt uses a different scenario.

If you are paying in instalments

First payment via Paystack. Remaining instalments arranged through our Slack community via MoMo.

  • Intermediate only — GHS 400 at enrollment, GHS 350 before Week 3.
  • Intermediate and assessment — GHS 400, then GHS 350, then GHS 300.
  • Payments do not extend beyond the five-week programme, and full payment is required before the certification assessment.
  • If an instalment is missed, access pauses until it is paid. We will always ask before that happens.

There is no automated instalment billing. You arrange each one with Johnathan directly, which also means a bad week is a conversation rather than a failed charge.
