What you have paid for

Your confirmation on WhatsApp will itemise it against what you chose at checkout.

The five-week Intermediate cohort, and — if you chose the bundle — one attempt at the BCAB assessment.

A readiness review: an advisory judgment on whether a BCAB attempt is realistic for you now. It is not a gate and cannot stop you sitting the assessment.

Training and certification are separate products. Completing the cohort qualifies you to attempt the assessment. It does not award the credential, and no payment can. The credential is earned by passing.

Path B exists so the credential does not depend on having bought our training, and you have not bought any. Nothing about the assessment is easier or harder for having come this way.

If you do not reach the standard first time you receive written feedback and an outcome of Assessment Not Yet Passed or Remediation Required, and you may reattempt. Every attempt uses a different scenario.
