Checking a claim before then

If someone has told you they hold a Bulletproof Automations credential and you want to confirm it, email info@bulletproofautomations.com with the name and we will tell you plainly.

Two things worth knowing while you wait. The Foundations Certificate is a certificate of completion — it records that someone finished the training and makes no independent competency claim. Only BCAB attests to assessed competence, and no one holds it yet. Anyone claiming otherwise today is mistaken.

Holders are also supplied with a permitted description of their credential and may not describe it as an n8n certification.
