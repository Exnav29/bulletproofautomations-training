Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Bulletproof Automations respects the privacy of individuals who visit our websites, enroll in our training programs, participate in our communities, apply for certification, or otherwise interact with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how Bulletproof Automations collects, uses, stores, discloses, and protects personal information in connection with the Bulletproof Automation Builder Pathway, our training programs, certification activities, websites, communities, and related services.

Bulletproof Automations is a United States-based company operating from Florida and serving learners, candidates, customers, and organizations in the United States and internationally.

By using our website or providing personal information to us, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with us.

Information you provide directly

We may collect:

your full name;
email address;
telephone or WhatsApp number;
country or general location;
employer or professional information that you choose to provide;
program or cohort selections;
payment option or payment-plan selections;
enrollment and attendance information;
portfolio submissions;
workflow exports, screenshots, documentation, and other training artifacts;
certification applications and assessment information;
communications sent through email, Slack, WhatsApp, forms, or other support channels;
feedback and survey responses;
testimonials, photographs, portfolio links, or other materials where you have agreed to provide them; and
other information you voluntarily submit.

We do not ask learners to provide passwords, API keys, private credentials, or unnecessary sensitive information as part of normal enrollment.

3. Payment information

Payments may be processed by third-party payment processors, including Paystack or other providers that Bulletproof Automations may use from time to time.

Bulletproof Automations may receive transaction-related information such as:

customer name;
email address;
transaction reference;
payment amount;
payment status;
program purchased; and
payment method category.

We generally do not receive or store full payment-card numbers when those details are entered directly into a payment processor’s systems.

Payment providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

When you use our websites, we or our infrastructure providers may automatically receive information such as:

IP address;
browser type;
device type;
operating system;
referring page or website;
pages visited;
approximate access time;
security and server-log information; and
other technical information reasonably necessary to operate and protect the website.

If we introduce analytics, cookies, advertising technology, or other forms of tracking that materially change the information we collect, we will update this Policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

process registrations and enrollments;

respond to questions and support requests;

These may include:

enrollment confirmations;
payment notices;
session reminders;
setup instructions;
deadline notifications;
assessment scheduling;
certification notices; and
important policy or program changes.

Depending on the information you provide and the program you join, we may communicate through email, WhatsApp, Slack, or another stated program channel.

Transactional or program-administration communications are not marketing communications and may continue even if you opt out of promotional messages.
