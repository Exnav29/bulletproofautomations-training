# Missed-course recovery policy

**Bulletproof Automations Training**  
**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective:** 31 October 2026  
**Owner:** Johnathan Lightfoot  
**Status:** Internal operating policy for instructor-approved recovery offers

## Purpose

Give a learner who paid for an instructor-led cohort but did not complete its learning requirements a reasonable way to finish, without charging the same cohort tuition twice, offering unlimited private teaching, or awarding a certificate for work not completed.

This policy supplements, and does not replace, the published [Refund and Cancellation Policy](https://training.bulletproofautomations.com/refund-policy), including its provisions on missed sessions, payment plans, and bundled training and assessments. It does not restrict rights provided by applicable law.

## Scope and eligibility

- Applies to paid Bulletproof Automations cohort training, including the five-week Intermediate programme, when the instructor approves a recovery opportunity in writing.
- Intended for learners who have incomplete course participation or missed learning activities, not for misconduct or assessment malpractice.
- The instructor first identifies which sessions, activities, or learning objectives remain incomplete. Do **not** assume that a paid registration or access to the materials constitutes completion.
- Each learner may select **one** of the recovery pathways below for the same incomplete cohort, with any later changes agreed in writing.
- This is a **one-time goodwill recovery opportunity** for that cohort, not an open-ended entitlement to repeated seats, private lessons, or unlimited review work.
- Any overdue original **training** tuition remains due. Completion recognition and certificate issuance require the original training balance to be paid in full.
- Do not disclose a learner's personal circumstances in public-facing communications or shared policy documents.

## Recovery pathways

### Option A: Next Intermediate cohort, no repeat tuition

- The learner may attend the **next equivalent Intermediate cohort that Bulletproof Automations actually schedules**, without paying the cohort tuition again.
- There is **no confirmed date or guarantee of when the next cohort will run**. Do not advertise a date or reserve a specific session until it is formally scheduled.
- The repeat seat is for that learner, for one subsequent cohort, subject to the course being offered. It is not cash, transferable tuition, or an indefinitely recurring seat.
- Instructor confirms completion against the programme's applicable completion expectations after the learner attends or makes up the required work.
- If a new cohort is not scheduled within the learner's practical timeframe, the learner may ask to switch to Option B or request a quote under Option C, without receiving a certificate prematurely.

**Additional recovery tuition:** GHS 0.

### Option B: Self-paced catch-up with an instructor review

- Provide available student guides, exercises, sample workflows, and other existing materials for the **specific missed modules**, at no additional course fee.
- Agree in writing on the missing learning activities to be completed and the evidence the learner should bring (for example, a workflow export or a short explanation of the result). This is a **completion check**, not a new or undisclosed certification examination.
- Include **one short review conversation**, arranged by mutual availability, to review the make-up work and resolve outstanding completion questions. Any additional tutoring beyond that review falls under Option C or a separately agreed service.
- A review meeting does not guarantee a certificate; the instructor must confirm that the missing requirements were completed.

**Additional recovery tuition:** GHS 0.

### Option C: Private instructor-led catch-up

- Offer **GHS 500 for up to three hours** of one-to-one instruction focused on the specifically identified missed course modules.
- A normal format is two 90-minute sessions; other divisions of the same total time may be agreed.
- Sessions are arranged at **mutually convenient** times. The learner does not receive unrestricted on-demand access to the instructor.
- Before collecting payment, record the missed modules, planned coverage, delivery format, and the agreed session times or scheduling approach.
- If the learner needs more than three hours to cover the gaps, explain that limitation **before payment**. Quote and obtain written agreement for any additional private hours separately. Never imply that GHS 500 buys however many hours are required.
- Completing the private sessions alone does not automatically confer a certificate; the instructor must confirm completion of the missed learning requirements.

**Additional private instruction fee:** GHS 500 for up to three hours. This fee pays for new individual instruction, not for the previously purchased cohort seat. The published refund policy and any written agreement govern changes or cancellations.

## Completion, certificates, and certification assessments

1. **Training completion:** The instructor records that the learner satisfied the missing requirements. Do not introduce retroactive grades or exams where none were part of the course.
2. **Payment verification:** Check that all required original course tuition has been received. Check any separate private-instruction fee before providing that purchased service.
3. **Certificate review:** A course **Certificate of Completion** may be prepared only after completion and payment have been verified and an authorized person approves issuance.
4. **Issuance controls:** Keep incomplete and payment-held learners out of the approved issuance batch. Unique certificate IDs are generated only through the normal Certificate Studio issuance process following final approval. Do **not** pre-issue certificates, IDs, PDFs, or emails.
5. **BCAB is separate:** Payment for a BCAB professional certification assessment, including a discounted training-and-assessment bundle, does not buy BCAB certification. Any unused assessment component remains subject to the published assessment, bundle, cancellation, and refund terms; it is not automatically converted to recovery tuition or silently forfeited.
6. **No employment promise:** Neither training completion nor BCAB certification guarantees employment.

## Administration and recordkeeping

For each recovery offer, keep a private learner record containing:

- Learner name and enrollment reference.
- Original course and cohort; original tuition paid, assessment component if any, and any remaining balance.
- Missing sessions/modules or completion requirements confirmed by the instructor.
- Date the three options were offered and the learner's written selection.
- For Option A: next cohort assignment and later attendance/completion confirmation.
- For Option B: materials provided, agreed make-up activities, review discussion, and completion decision.
- For Option C: scope, written GHS 500 agreement, actual instructional time delivered, and completion decision.
- Instructor's final completion decision, payment verification, Certificate Studio approval, and issuance details **only when issued**.

Keep this information in the restricted student administration system, **not** in the public GitHub repository. Communications should remain respectful and should not describe an incomplete learner as a failed certification candidate.

## Relationship to other policies

- The published [Refund and Cancellation Policy](https://training.bulletproofautomations.com/refund-policy) controls refund and cancellation requests and applicable statutory rights. This document adds an instructor-approved recovery option, not an automatic refund.
- The [Bulletproof Automation Builder Pathway competency framework](../competency-framework.md) governs professional certification requirements and the separation of training from BCAB assessment.
- Where this policy and a learner's existing written agreement differ, refer the issue to the programme owner before making a new promise.

## Implementation checklist

- [ ] Verify the incomplete modules and the student's enrollment/payment position.
- [ ] Offer all three options in writing, with a clear statement that no future cohort date is confirmed.
- [ ] Record the selected pathway and the scope of recovery.
- [ ] Deliver and verify the recovery work.
- [ ] Confirm original training fees are fully paid.
- [ ] Approve the training-completion decision.
- [ ] Only then follow Certificate Studio's controlled issuance workflow.
